Faculty are reminded that the state-aligned unit "Our World Today: Flags and
Nations of Modern Europe" (Heartland Educational Publishing) is approved for
classroom use beginning the week of September 13.

Teachers should review the unit's discussion questions in advance. Question 2
of "Think & Discuss" invites comparison of housing arrangements. In light of
recent events, and given the number of families in our parish with relatives
in New Orleans, teachers are asked to use their judgment before opening this
question to general discussion, and to notify parents by the usual note home
where appropriate.

Counselors remain available on the ordinary schedule.

This memo replaces the verbal guidance given at the August in-service. Please
retain a copy with your unit materials.
