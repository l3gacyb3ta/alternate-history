# factbook

**Status: specified, not yet measured.** Derived from millmint.net/factbook and
the invented-vexillology sheet.

Job: a reference work about something that does not exist. Encyclopedia,
gazetteer, program lore, an in-world manual for a YSWS.

This is the only mode with an ornament budget, and the budget has one
condition: **the ornament must be diegetic.** It is produced by the world, not
applied to the page.

The mechanism, measured on millmint: it forks the `flag-icons` library and
defines **225 flags — 108 real ISO codes and 117 invented polities**
(`fi-lucayoneque`, `fi-soualiga`, `fi-guanima`) sitting in the same namespace as
`fi-gb` and `fi-jp`, with no separation between real and fictional. The fiction
is not styling laid over a website; it is the website's data model. Likewise its
typefaces — `bamum`, `nay aug black`, `whirly birdie`, `whirlybats` — are the
world's typefaces, loaded as `@font-face`, over a system-sans body.

So:
- Display faces are permitted here and only here, for headings and dingbats.
  Body text is still system sans at 16/1.5. No exceptions.
- Real and invented entities share one vocabulary and one visual grammar.
- Infoboxes, breadcrumbs, plate numbers, cross-references, "see also". The
  apparatus of a reference work is the aesthetic.
- Palette discipline is *tighter* here than elsewhere, not looser: the flag
  sheet holds ~six flats across two dozen designs. A shared limited palette is
  what makes a set of invented things look like they come from one place.
