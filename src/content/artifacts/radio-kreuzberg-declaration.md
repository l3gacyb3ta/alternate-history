**ANNOUNCER:** — still with us, good. The transmitter has held. For those
just finding the frequency: it is a quarter past nine, it is snowing, and the
council of councils is still voting. We can see the hall from this window.
The lights are on in every floor.

**ANNOUNCER:** We are told the count is nearly finished. We are told —

*[several seconds of crowd noise]*

**ANNOUNCER:** That is not from the hall. That is the square below us. I will
hold the microphone out the window and you will hear what I hear.

*[thirty-one seconds: crowd, bells, a brass band beginning and stopping]*

**SPEAKER (from the hall, relayed):** — that the city of Berlin, whole and
undivided, assumes from this night the government of itself; that it asks
nothing of any state but a road, a railway, and the ordinary courtesy of
neighbors; and that its treasury, its housing, and its bread are from this
night the common property of Berliners —

**ANNOUNCER:** They are reading it aloud twice. Once for the hall, once for
the square.

**ANNOUNCER:** Stay by your radios. There is a great deal of winter left.
