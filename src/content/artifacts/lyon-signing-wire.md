LYON 18 MAY 1615H

TREATY SIGNED AT SILK EXCHANGE 1450H STOP ALL DELEGATIONS PRESENT STOP

CEREMONY DELAYED FORTY MINUTES OWING TO CROWD ON THE QUAYS ESTIMATED BY
PREFECTURE AT ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND STOP DELEGATES OBLIGED TO ENTER BY THE
RIVER DOOR STOP NO INCIDENTS STOP

WEAVERS ASSOCIATION PRESENTED EACH SIGNATORY WITH A WOVEN PANEL GOLD ON RED
STOP MINISTER ACCEPTED AND WORE IT STOP PHOTOGRAPHS FOLLOW BY COURIER STOP

FULL PROTOCOL TEXT FOLLOWS BY DIPLOMATIC BAG STOP

ENDS
